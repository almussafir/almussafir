Privacy Policy – Al-Mussafir

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Al-Mussafir (“the app”) is a travel and worship companion for Umrah. This policy explains what information the app uses and what it does not do.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves a few settings on your device only (in your browser's local storage). This information is never sent to us or to any other server:

  • Your chosen language and whether you have seen the welcome screen.
  • The gate number and hotel name you entered in “Find my way”.
  • The Tawaf and Sa'i counter, reminders, Rawdah reservation time, and currency choices.
  • Your saved favourites and Dhikr progress.
  • The last exchange rates the app downloaded, so they can be shown offline.

You can remove this information at any time by clearing the website data in your browser or uninstalling the app.

2. Location

The app asks for your location only when you tap a button that needs it: the Qibla compass and “My location” on the map. Your location is used at that moment to calculate the direction or to show your position on the map. It is not stored and not sent to us.

3. Information sent to other services

Some features load live information from third-party services. When you use them, the service may receive your IP address and the request itself, as with any website:

  • Prayer times: Aladhan (api.aladhan.com), using city names.
  • Weather: Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com), using the coordinates of Makkah and Medina.
  • Exchange rates: open.er-api.com and jsDelivr (currency-api).
  • Maps: OpenStreetMap and Google Maps.
  • Magnetic declination (Qibla): NOAA, using your approximate coordinates.
  • Live streams: YouTube, only when you open a live stream.
  • Fonts: Google Fonts.

These services have their own privacy policies.

4. What we do not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up.
  • No advertising and no tracking.
  • No analytics about your use of the app.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • We do not collect your name, phone number, email, or passport details.

5. Donations

The Sadaqah section links to external donation pages (currently ehsan.sa). Any payment is made on that website, and that site's privacy policy applies. Al-Mussafir does not receive or handle your payment details.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and the date above will be changed.
